Training Callbacks

EarlyStopping

Monitors val_loss, patience=5, restores best weights. Prevents overfitting by stopping training when validation loss stops improving.

ReduceLROnPlateau

Monitors val_loss, factor=0.5, patience=3, min_lr=1e-7. Reduces learning rate by half when validation loss plateaus.

ModelCheckpoint

Monitors val_accuracy, saves best model only. Ensures the final model is the one with highest validation accuracy.
